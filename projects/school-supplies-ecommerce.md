## Overview
A custom e-commerce product for a specific consumer segment. It includes a CRM for clients and orders, a product-management interface for uploading products and managing stock, and vendor onboarding and accounting. It spans a web portal, backend portal, and a mobile application.

- Worked on the backend portal for users.
