# Job sites and channels for a Pakistan-based senior .NET/Azure/AI engineer seeking visa-sponsored relocation (late 2026)

Research depth note: about 14 tool calls. Search-engine summaries were the main evidence, and several primary pages were only partly read. Most "effectiveness" numbers come from vendor blogs and are low confidence. Platform-by-platform visa-filter features (Indeed, Glassdoor, StepStone, Xing, Seek, Bayt, etc.) were NOT verified and are listed under Gaps.

## 1. How the big general platforms compare for visa-sponsored tech roles; which have visa/relocation filters

### Takeaway
I could not verify filter features for most platforms. The only platform-level data I found is a weak LinkedIn vs Google Jobs response-rate comparison (vendor-sourced). Practical routing from the sources: use the official government portal and sponsor registers to pick employers, then apply on the employer's site.

### Cited Findings
- Huntr-attributed figures (2026, "600k+ applications"): Google Jobs 11.29% response rate, LinkedIn 3.10%; Indeed and FlexJobs listed N/A. Methodology and the "response" definition are not stated, and the article links to a Boston University Questrom blog rather than the original Huntr report. LOW confidence — [bestjobsearchapps.com](https://bestjobsearchapps.com/articles/en/best-job-sites-for-easy-applications-in-2026-high-response-rates-from-600k-apps)
- For Germany, third-party guides list Make-it-in-Germany.com (federal government portal for international skilled workers, with a job portal), LinkedIn, StepStone.de and Xing.de. Secondary-source claim — [wheretoemigrate/other guides via search](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/service/newsletter-no-4-/-2026) (the Make it in Germany "IT staff from abroad" newsletter exists; its content was not read)
- One guide suggests searching LinkedIn with "visa sponsorship" or "relocation assistance" in quotes, and the UK Find a Job service for "visa sponsorship". Single low-quality source, anecdotal in nature — [Blind/Fishbowl/aggregator results](https://www.teamblind.com/post/which-countries-sponsor-a-visa-for-entry-level-swes-txzalxgn)
- Blind and Fishbowl threads show people asking which platform yields sponsored offers, but I found no reliable answer, and no Reddit/HN first-hand report from a Pakistani engineer. Anecdotal — [Blind thread](https://www.teamblind.com/post/looking-for-a-job-in-canadaeurope-rhvi5joo)
- Germany: the Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card) allows up to 12 months in Germany to job-hunt, and Pakistan-specific coverage reports heavy demand and embassy delays — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1045607); [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/?p=1026181)

### Inferences
- Because no Easy-Apply-style platform has a verified, reliable "sponsors visas" filter, a sponsor-register-first workflow (question 5) is likely more dependable than platform filters, whose "visa sponsorship" tags are employer self-declared.
- For a senior profile, GulfTalent, Bayt and Naukrigulf are Gulf-focused and are likely relevant only if the Gulf is a target; this is not verified here.

### Gaps
- No verified information on whether LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Wellfound, Welcome to the Jungle, StepStone, Xing, Jobs.ie/IrishJobs, Totaljobs, Seek, GulfTalent, Bayt, Naukrigulf or Jobserve have visa/relocation filters or how well they perform for sponsored applicants. Needs platform help pages.
- No Pakistan-specific first-hand reports (Reddit/HN) found.

## 2. Relocation-specific boards: alive, reputable, useful; pricing

### Takeaway
Relocate.me (The Global Move) is live with a paid tier; Landing.jobs was acquired in April 2025 and Honeypot was acquired by New Work in 2019. Status of most other named boards is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Relocate.me runs "The Global Move": a weekly hand-picked list of tech jobs with visa/relocation support. Publisher claims one 2026 edition had 160 jobs (115 relocation-friendly, 45 international remote); free subscribers get resources and visa updates. Publisher's own claim — [Relocate.me](https://relocate.me/companies-hiring/the-global-move); [Substack](https://relocateme.substack.com/p/weekly-hand-curated-tech-jobs-with-034?open=false)
- Paid tier is described in search snippets as $12/month; the Relocate.me page itself shows no price and only lists "1700+ Curated Visa Sponsorship and Remote Tech Jobs (Paid Option)". The $12 figure is unconfirmed on a primary page — [Relocate.me](https://relocate.me/companies-hiring/the-global-move); [search snippet source](https://relocate.me/remote/remote/the-global-move/unlock-500-tech-jobs-with-visa-sponsorship-paid-option-10080)
- Relocate.me also advertises live Q&A sessions with recruiters and hiring managers — [Relocate.me](https://relocate.me/companies-hiring/the-global-move)
- Arbeitnow: Germany-focused board launched 2021 with visa-sponsorship and remote tags plus relocation guides; it is integrated with Greenhouse's job-posting integrations — [Arbeitnow](https://www.arbeitnow.com); [Greenhouse integration](https://www.greenhouse.com/integrations/arbeitnow)
- Landing.jobs: acquired by Damia Group Portugal in April 2025 (CB Insights) — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/honeypot-vs-landingjobs)
- Honeypot: acquired by New Work (XING's parent) in April 2019 (CB Insights) — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/honeypot-vs-landingjobs)
- Jobbatical: CB Insights lists "Acquired" with no date; a Seedtable page shows a company filing dated 25 Aug 2026. Status conflicting/unclear — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/jobbatical-vs-landingjobs); [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/jobbatical)
- Himalayas: no sign of shutdown; described as a remote-first board founded 2021 — [Apify listing](https://apify.com/himalayas)
- Duoflag "Visa Sponsors Weekly" Substack newsletter lists sponsor roles in UK, Germany and the Netherlands; partly paywalled, freshness unknown — [Duoflag](https://duoflag.substack.com/p/visa-sponsors-weekly-full)

### Inferences
- Paid curated lists are low-cost relative to the relocation stakes, but the value claims are publisher-made; treat as a lead source, not a guarantee, and verify each employer on the official register.

### Gaps
- Unverified: Berlin Startup Jobs, Working Nomads, Remote OK, We Work Remotely, TechPays, Wellfound current status, Jobbatical current operation, Landing.jobs post-acquisition quality. Not searched or no results.
- Exact current Global Move price and refund terms not confirmed from a primary page.

## 3. Response/interview rates: Easy Apply vs direct vs referral vs recruiter outreach; automation and ToS risk

### Takeaway
Referrals are the strongest-evidenced channel (Ashby: about 40% vs 3% interview rate, via secondary sources). Easy Apply vs direct figures are inconsistent vendor claims. LinkedIn's own help page prohibits auto-apply bots and extensions.

### Cited Findings
- Ashby dataset (38M+ applications, 2021–2024, as cited by secondary sources): 40% of referred candidates reached interview vs 3% of cold applicants. Original report not read — [Dataford](https://dataford.io/blog/do-referrals-help-2026); [DataDriven](https://datadriven.io/blog/cold-de-applications-are-dead-in-2026-use-referrals)
- Unsourced: referred candidates hired at 30% vs 7% for job-board applicants (DataDriven gives no source). LOW confidence — [DataDriven](https://datadriven.io/blog/cold-de-applications-are-dead-in-2026-use-referrals)
- Easy Apply vs direct: vendor blogs claim 1–3% (Easy Apply) vs 3–5% or 15–30% (direct); roughly 100–200 Easy Apply applications per offer vs 30–60 direct. Numbers inconsistent across sources, vendor-produced, no independent dataset found. LOW confidence — [Careery](https://careery.pro/blog/job-boards-vs-company-website-which-is-better); [LoopCV](https://www.loopcv.pro/tr/guides/is-linkedin-easy-apply-worth-it); [FastApply](https://blog.fastapply.co/linkedin-easy-apply-vs-direct-company-applications-2026)
- Huntr-attributed: LinkedIn 3.10%, Google Jobs 11.29% — see Q1 for caveats — [bestjobsearchapps.com](https://bestjobsearchapps.com/articles/en/best-job-sites-for-easy-applications-in-2026-high-response-rates-from-600k-apps)
- LinkedIn Help (primary): third-party software, bots, browser plug-ins and extensions that scrape or automate activity are prohibited under User Agreement Section 8.2; members may have accounts restricted or shut down, and tools may stop working without notice — [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387)
- Enforcement severity is disputed: one vendor says most cases are temporary Easy Apply pauses; another cites a 70–85% ban rate for tools like LazyApply with no source. LOW confidence — [FastApply](https://blog.fastapply.co/can-you-get-banned-for-auto-applying-on-linkedin-2026); [Scale.jobs](https://scale.jobs/blog/lazyapply-risk-profile-banned-linkedin)
- Press coverage notes AI bots flooding LinkedIn with applications — [CO/AI](https://getcoai.com/news/ai-bots-are-flooding-linkedin-with-job-applications/)
- Glassdoor blog on whether AI has killed online applications — headline only, content not read — [Glassdoor](https://static.glassdoor.ca/blog/ai-has-not-killed-online-job-applications/)

### Inferences
- A senior engineer needing sponsorship has strong reason to avoid bulk auto-apply: a restricted LinkedIn account is costly, and sponsored roles are screened more carefully.
- Referral/recruiter-warm routes plausibly matter more for non-local candidates because of the sponsorship filter, but no data isolates visa-seeking candidates.

### Gaps
- No data on response rates specifically for international or sponsorship-needing applicants.
- Indeed's automation/ToS position not retrieved.
- No data on recruiter outreach (inbound) conversion rates.

## 4. Recruiters/agencies, EOR-based remote hiring (Deel, Remote, Oyster), remote companies hiring in Pakistan

### Takeaway
EOR platforms do cover Pakistan, but the evidence is vendor-written. Which specific remote employers hire in Pakistan is largely unverified; remote-from-Pakistan is a separate path from visa relocation.

### Cited Findings
- Remote's Pakistan page states it has its own legal entity in Pakistan (vendor claim) — [Remote](https://remote.com/ko-kr/country-explorer/pakistan/employer-of-record)
- EOR pricing claims conflict: Deel about $599/employee/month vs Oyster about $699 in comparison sites; contractor fees quoted at differing amounts. Affiliate/vendor content, LOW confidence — [CostBench](https://costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-oyster-hr/); [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com/resources/workforce-management/employer-of-record/best-eor-in-pakistan/)
- A social-media compilation lists Automattic and GitLab as "Pakistan Confirmed"; both describe themselves as fully distributed. Not an official source — LOW confidence — [LinkedIn post](https://ph.linkedin.com/in/mbaliber)
- Jobgether has Pakistan-eligible remote listings, including an EMEA/Pakistan role at OKX — [Jobgether](https://jobgether.com/remote-jobs/pakistan/tech/2)

### Inferences
- Candidates pay nothing for EOR; the hiring company does. Whether a company hires in Pakistan depends on its own policy (often via contractor arrangement), not on the platform alone.
- Remote-from-Karachi jobs are a fallback or income bridge but do not themselves deliver a visa.

### Gaps
- No verified list of remote companies that employ in Pakistan; no reliable evidence about agency/recruiter effectiveness for Pakistan-based candidates; no evidence on Pakistan-specific tax/contractor issues.

## 5. Official sponsor registers and how to use them

### Takeaway
The UK and Dutch registers are public and are the best way to filter employers. The UK register was updated 196 times in 2026 through 2 November, so always download a fresh copy.

### Cited Findings
- UK register of licensed sponsors (Worker and Temporary Worker routes), published by UKVI, first published 13 Nov 2013, last updated 2 Nov 2026, with 196 update entries from 2 Jan to 2 Nov 2026 — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers)
- Netherlands: the highly skilled migrant permit requires the employer to be an IND recognised sponsor, listed in a public register; the legal employer on the contract must match the register entry (some groups register one entity and offer from another) — [ElyForma via search](https://elyforma.com/blog/netherlands-highly-skilled-migrant-visa)
- Netherlands 2026 HSM salary thresholds (gross/month, excl. 8% holiday allowance): age 30+ €5,942; under 30 €4,357; reduced €3,122 — [Grant Thornton NL](https://www.grantthornton.nl/en/insights-en/human-capital-services/2026-salary-threshold-changes); [Meijburg](https://www.meijburg.com/news/annual-adjustment-salary-criterion-highly-skilled-migrants-2026). Access Financial quotes 2025 figures (€5,688 etc.), which are outdated.
- Germany Blue Card: sources conflict on 2026 thresholds (about €50,700 standard and €45,934 IT/shortage, vs €45,300 general); an IT specialist route without degree needs 3 years' experience in the last 7 years — [Immigrant Spirit](https://www.immigrantspirit.com/eu-blue-card-opportunity-card-requirements/); [Abroadmate](https://abroadmate.me/guides/eu-blue-card-germany). Verify at Make it in Germany or BAMF.

### Inferences
- Workflow: download the UK register, filter by town/city and sector-relevant names, cross-check against careers pages for .NET/Azure roles, and apply directly or approach engineers there for referral. Do the same with the IND list.
- A senior .NET/Azure/AI salary should clear the Dutch and German thresholds easily; the binding constraint is employer willingness, not salary.

### Gaps
- IND register page and GOV.UK register file fields (CSV columns, route/rating) not read. UK Skilled Worker salary thresholds and 2026 rule changes not covered. Ireland (critical skills employment permit), Canada, Australia, Gulf and other registers not covered.
