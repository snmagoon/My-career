# Work permits and tech hiring: Nordics, Baltics, CEE, Southern Europe (non-EU senior .NET/Azure/AI engineer, Pakistani national, as of Nov 2026)

Research status: about 16 search/fetch calls, hit by a search rate limit. Coverage is uneven. Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway and Czechia have official or near-official thresholds. The Baltics, Spain, Portugal and Malta have partial data from secondary sources. Poland has only general framework info. Romania, Hungary, Italy, Greece and Cyprus were NOT researched (no usable results), so there are no sourced facts for them. Tech-market and salary data (question 2) is almost entirely missing. Everything below is dated as found. Secondary sources (law firms, relocation blogs, aggregators such as visaatlas.org) should be verified on official sites.

## Q1. Main permit route, salary thresholds, processing times, sponsor requirements, recent policy changes

### Takeaway
Nordic thresholds (2026) are Sweden SEK 34,470/month, Denmark DKK 552,000/year (about EUR 74K), Finland EUR 3,937/month, Norway NOK 545,400 (bachelor) or 624,700 (master) per year. Czechia's EU Blue Card needs CZK 73,823/month. Spain, Portugal, Malta and the Baltics have lower or faster routes, but the figures are less well verified.

### Cited Findings

#### Sweden
- From 1 June 2026, the work-permit salary must be at least 90% of the SCB median salary at the time of application. This applies to first-time applicants who receive a decision on or after 1 June. — [Migrationsverket, new rules](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/news-archive-for-employers/news/2026-06-01-new-rules-for-work-permits-come-into-force.html)
- Extensions applied for between 1 June and 1 Dec 2026 stay under the old 80% rule; from 2 Dec 2026 the new threshold applies. — [Migrationsverket](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/news-archive-for-employers/news/2026-06-01-new-rules-for-work-permits-come-into-force.html)
- A 75% tier applies to exempt groups, including a government list of occupations that names IT technicians and systems administrators, student/researcher permit holders applying for the first time, and employees of start-ups (under 5 years old, fewer than 100 staff, tech/life-science). — [Migrationsverket](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/news-archive-for-employers/news/2026-06-01-new-rules-for-work-permits-come-into-force.html)
- Per a web-search summary of Migrationsverket's page, the median was SEK 38,300 from 16 June 2026, giving a minimum of SEK 34,470/month. Earlier secondary sources cite SEK 33,390, now outdated. Conflict flagged; I did not fetch the 16 June page directly. — [Migrationsverket salary requirements](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/word-explanations/salary-requirements-for-a-work-permit.html); outdated figure in [EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/sweden-to-raise-work-permit-salary-threshold-to-sek-33390-in-june-2026/)
- EU Blue Card validity rose from 2 to 4 years from 1 June 2026. — [Migrationsverket](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/news-archive-for-employers/news/2026-06-01-new-rules-for-work-permits-come-into-force.html)
- Employers must notify the agency if employment has not started within four months of a permit being granted. The agency may reject applications over employer problems such as sanctions for unlawful employment or tax deficiencies. — [Migrationsverket](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/news-archive-for-employers/news/2026-06-01-new-rules-for-work-permits-come-into-force.html)
- Sweden resumed residence and work permit processing in Pakistan in April 2025 (previously handled via Addis Ababa), with in-person steps at the Islamabad embassy. — [Envoy Global](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/sweden-resumes-residence-and-work-permit-processing-in-pakistan/)

#### Denmark
- Pay Limit Scheme 2026: DKK 552,000/year (2025: DKK 514,000). Supplementary Pay Limit Scheme: DKK 446,000 (2025: 415,000). Same thresholds apply to the salary track of the Fast-track Scheme. Applications after 1 Jan 2026. — [Conventus Law / Bird & Bird](https://conventuslaw.com/report/denmark-foreign-labour-new-salary-thresholds-under-the-pay-limit-schemes-and-updated-positive-lists/)
- Only base salary, employer pension contributions and holiday allowance count; bonuses and taxable benefits do not. — [same](https://conventuslaw.com/report/denmark-foreign-labour-new-salary-thresholds-under-the-pay-limit-schemes-and-updated-positive-lists/)
- Positive Lists are updated 1 Jan and 1 July; as of 1 Jan 2026 the Higher Education list had 183 titles and the Skilled Workers list 57, both slightly reduced. The article does not say whether IT titles are on them. — [same](https://conventuslaw.com/report/denmark-foreign-labour-new-salary-thresholds-under-the-pay-limit-schemes-and-updated-positive-lists/)
- Application fee DKK 6,810 per permit (secondary source). — [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/23/denmark-sets-minimum-salary-for-foreign-workers/) (search snippet only)
- Processing for Pakistan-based applicants: only an undated consultancy claim of about 1 month; unreliable. — [MRC Pakistan](https://www.mrc.org.pk/en/resources/visa-information/denmark-visa-regimes/preview)

#### Finland
- Specialist permit (also Blue Card): gross salary at least EUR 3,937/month in 2026 (2025: 3,827); fringe benefits excluded. Below threshold, the case falls to the slower employed-person permit. — [Migri income requirement](https://migri.fi/working-in-finland/income-requirement), [Migri specialist](https://migri.fi/en/specialist)
- Fee EUR 530 online (was 380) from 1 Jan 2026 (secondary). — [Rivermate](https://www.rivermate.com/guides/finland/work-permits-and-visas)
- Processing time 8-16 weeks per unofficial guide only; no official Migri figure found. — [workvisa.guide](https://workvisa.guide/philippines/finland)

#### Norway
- Skilled worker permit floors where no collective agreement applies: NOK 545,400/year (bachelor-level) and NOK 624,700/year (master-level). Lower pay accepted only with strong evidence it is normal for the occupation. — [VisaAtlas, Pakistan to Norway](https://visaatlas.org/from/pakistani/to/norway/skilled-worker-permit) (secondary, review dates inconsistent)
- UDI reportedly raised minimum levels from 1 Sept 2025. — [Lingu.no](https://lingu.no/posts/norway-work-visa-requirements-2026-documents-salary-thresholds-and-approval-tips) (secondary)
- Processing time: sources conflict (1-3 months vs 2-3 months for degree-level vs "no central figure"); Pakistani applicants file through a Norwegian consulate/VAC, and NOKUT degree evaluation can take 2-3 months. — [VisaAtlas](https://visaatlas.org/from/pakistani/to/norway/skilled-worker-permit)

#### Estonia / Latvia / Lithuania
- Estonia: 2026 employer guide lists a 1.5x average-salary coefficient for top specialists, about EUR 3,138 gross/month, for applications from 5 March 2026. ERR reported a government proposal to cut the top-specialist threshold from 2x to 1.5x; status unclear. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/estonia-temporary-residence-permit-employer-guide), [ERR](https://news.err.ee/1608489296/sports)
- Latvia: about EUR 1,685/month for an employment permit and about EUR 2,528 for Blue Card, per an aggregator. Unverified. — [atozserwisplus](https://www.atozserwisplus.com/salary/latvia/2026)
- Lithuania: sources conflict between 1.2x average gross salary for ICT/high-value roles and 1.5x for highly qualified specialists; undated. — [workinlithuania.com](https://workinlithuania.com/?p=2747); [migration.lt](https://www.migration.lt/?p=2345)

#### Poland
- A new foreign-employment law took effect 1 June 2025; Type A permits require a Poland-registered employer and applications are online via praca.gov.pl. — [Centuro Global](https://www.centuroglobal.com/article/poland-immigration-policy-2025/), [Careers in Poland](https://www.careersinpoland.com/article/news/new-regulations-for-foreign-workers-in-poland-in-2026)
- August 2026 tightening (visa required before entry for work; no work-permit work on visa-free stay) applies to Colombia, Georgia and Venezuela only, not Pakistan. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/14/poland-tightens-work-visa-rules-for-specific-countries/)
- EY (23 July 2026) reports a draft with new work-permit exemptions and employer compliance measures; details not retrieved. — [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1593-poland-proposes-new-work-permit-exemptions-and-employer-compliance-measures)
- Poland's EU Blue Card salary threshold was not found.

#### Czechia
- EU Blue Card minimum CZK 73,823 gross/month from 1 May 2026 to 30 April 2027 (1.5x average wage; previously CZK 69,248). One outlier source says 73,833. — [Envoy Global](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/czechia-eu-blue-card-salary-threshold-increases-2026/), [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/news/czechia-raises-eu-blue-card-salary-threshold-launches-sweeping-immigration-overhaul/)
- Employee Card threshold unclear (market/guaranteed wage vs minimum wage CZK 22,400 from Jan 2026). — [EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/czech-republic-to-raise-salary-thresholds-for-blue-card-and-ict-permits-in-2026/)

#### Spain
- Highly Qualified Professional (HQP)/Blue Card salary: most sources say about EUR 41,356 general and EUR 33,085 reduced (updated June 2026 per one source); older pages say EUR 27,000. Conflict; verify. — [Migratiolex](https://migratiolex.com/minimum-wage-pac-visa-spain/), [VisaAtlas](https://visaatlas.org/salary-thresholds/spain)
- Processing: about 20 working days for UGE-CE authorisation; one July 2026 source says 3-6 weeks. Degree or 3+ years experience needed. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/spain-highly-qualified-professionals-permit)

#### Portugal
- Highly qualified route needs salary of about 1.5x national average gross; AIMA reference figure EUR 2,157/month (2023-based); Blue Card figures vary by source (EUR 21,030/year vs EUR 3,480-3,680/month). Conflict. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/portugal-eu-blue-card-salary-thresholds)
- Tech Visa is a fast-track within the highly qualified route for IAPMEI-certified employers. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/portugal-work-permits-hr-guide)
- Job-seeker visa now limited to highly qualified professionals. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/portugal-new-immigration-rules-business)
- AIMA backlogs: no authoritative current data; indicative 30-60 days (unofficial). — [IBA](https://prod-bo.ibanet.org/portugal-immigration-bottleneck) (title only seen)

#### Malta
- Key Employee Initiative (KEI): most recent sources say EUR 45,000/year gross for managerial/highly technical roles; older sources say 30-35K. Fast-track about 5 working days vs 6-12 weeks for the standard single permit. Official Identita FAQ exists but was not read. — [Identita FAQ PDF](https://identita.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/FAQs-Highly-Qualified-1.pdf), [Dixcart](https://www.dixcart.com/?p=12652)

### Inferences
- For a EUR 72-100K role, Sweden (SEK 34,470/month, about EUR 3.1K), Finland, Norway, Czechia, Estonia, Spain and Malta thresholds are all comfortably cleared. Denmark's DKK 552K (about EUR 74K) is the only threshold close to the bottom of the range, so a Danish offer would need to be near the top of it.
- Sweden's IT-technician exemption (75%) probably does not apply to a senior engineer role; this is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- Romania, Hungary, Italy, Greece, Cyprus: no sourced data (search rate limit hit). Not researched.
- Official processing times for Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway (Pakistan-specific).
- Poland and Latvia/Lithuania official thresholds; Norway official page not fetched.
- Sponsor/employer registration requirements (e.g. Denmark SIRI, Sweden certified employer, Finland, Estonia) not verified.

## Q2. Tech hiring climate 2026, salary ranges, relocation norms

### Takeaway
Almost no usable 2026 evidence was found; only rough salary aggregates.

### Cited Findings
- Poland: Payscale average senior .NET base PLN 184,172 (10th-90th percentile PLN 36K-306K). Not converted to EUR. — [Payscale PL](https://www.payscale.com/research/PL/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary)
- Portugal: Payscale average senior .NET base EUR 31,907 (range EUR 20K-56K). — [Payscale PT](https://www.payscale.com/research/PT/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary)
- Qubit Labs (undated, USD): senior .NET averages of $84K Poland/Spain/Portugal, $93.6K Sweden, $124.8K Denmark. — [Qubit Labs](https://qubit-labs.com/average-net-developer-salary-in-the-world/)

### Inferences
- If these aggregates are right, EUR 72-100K is plausible in Denmark and Sweden (and Norway) but unlikely as an average in Poland, Portugal or Spain, where it would be a top-of-market outcome. This is weakly supported.

### Gaps
- Demand for .NET/Azure/AI, English-language job ads, architect/TPM demand, relocation package norms, Levels.fyi data, EUR salary ranges for Czechia, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Finland, Norway, Baltics: not found.

## Q3. Realism for a Pakistani national needing sponsorship; nationality-specific issues

### Takeaway
No Pakistan-specific work-permit bans were found in the researched countries, but processing is consular and can be slow. Specifics are thin.

### Cited Findings
- Poland's August 2026 restrictions do not name Pakistan. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/14/poland-tightens-work-visa-rules-for-specific-countries/)
- Norway: no nationality-specific treaty framework for Pakistanis; filing is at a Norwegian consulate/VAC in Pakistan, so appointment backlogs can lengthen waits; TB test requirement unconfirmed. — [VisaAtlas](https://visaatlas.org/from/pakistani/to/norway/skilled-worker-permit)
- Sweden processes permits in Pakistan since April 2025; document inconsistencies cause delays and refusals (consultancy claim). — [Envoy Global](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/sweden-resumes-residence-and-work-permit-processing-in-pakistan/), [VisaProcessInfos](https://www.visaprocessinfos.com/blog/sweden-work-visa-from-pakistan-2026)
- 2023 Schengen short-stay visa approval rate for Pakistani applicants was 47.1% (short-stay, not work permits). — [VisaProcessInfos](https://www.visaprocessinfos.com/nationality/pakistani/sweden)

### Inferences
- Because Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Norway all require thresholds the candidate would likely meet, the main risk is consular delay and job-offer availability, not eligibility. Fast-track options (Denmark Fast-track Scheme, Malta KEI, Portugal Tech Visa, Spain HQP) are worth targeting, but their Pakistan-specific timings are unverified.

### Gaps
- Nationality-specific slowdowns or enhanced screening for Pakistan in Denmark, Finland, Baltics, Czechia, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Greece, Cyprus, Malta, Romania, Hungary: not found.
- Czechia and Poland consular wait times for Pakistan (Czech embassy appointment system known to be a bottleneck in general, but not researched here).
