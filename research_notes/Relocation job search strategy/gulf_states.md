# Gulf (ex-UAE) tech hiring and expat packages for a Pakistani senior .NET/Azure/AI engineer, as of Nov 2026

Research quality warning: the search tools returned mostly aggregators, recruiter blogs and job boards. Several primary sources (Fragomen, Gulf Business) returned 403. No official government, Hays or Michael Page salary tables were retrievable. Treat all figures as rough, and verify visa status with official channels before acting.

## 1. Demand in 2026 (cloud, AI/GenAI, .NET, tech leads, TPM) and employers

### Takeaway
Demand for AI, cloud and data specialists is described as strong across the GCC, and Saudi Arabia is the focal market (Humain, Vision 2030). I found no reliable hard demand numbers for the other four countries or for .NET/TPM specifically.

### Cited Findings
- Hays GCC Salary Guide 2026: demand is highest for technical and digital specialists, leadership roles and flexible project staffing. 66% of employers grew headcount in 2025 and only 13% reported no major hiring plans for 2026. 90% reported skills gaps. GCC GDP growth for 2026 projected at 4.6%. Publication date not stated (copyright 2026). — [Zawya, Hays press release](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/research-studies/hays-middle-east-releases-hays-gcc-salary-guide-2026-369616)
- Hays: in Saudi Arabia 93% of employers employ Saudi nationals and 75% plan to increase Saudi headcount in 2026, which is a localisation headwind for expats. — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/research-studies/hays-middle-east-releases-hays-gcc-salary-guide-2026-369616)
- Humain is a PIF-backed AI company launched in May 2025 (flagship Arabic LLM "ALLaM"). One outlook claims it secured up to USD 1.2bn for AI infrastructure; this is unverified. — [Wikipedia: Humain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humain); [Nimbus](https://nimbusconsultancy.com/ksa/company-formation-in-the-ksa-2026-business-setup-saudi-arabia/)
- Commentary says Saudi AI infrastructure investment is creating demand for AI engineers, data and cloud specialists, and that specialised foreign experts remain in demand despite Saudization (low-authority blogs). — [Zerotaxjobs](https://zerotaxjobs.com/articles/how-artificial-intelligence-jobs-in-saudi-arabia-will-benefit-from-investment); [Saviorhire](https://www.saviorhire.com/post/tech-hiring-trends-in-saudi-arabia)
- Michael Page: 79% of Saudi professionals reported actively seeking new roles (headline only; date not verified). — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/jobs/michael-page-79-of-saudi-professionals-actively-seeking-new-roles)
- A foundit Gulf listing page showed about 3,428 IT jobs across the Gulf on 23 Oct 2026, but it includes the UAE and I could not split by country. — [foundit Gulf](https://www.founditgulf.com/search/it-jobs)
- A claim of about 45,000 active GCC tech openings and 60% YoY IT hiring growth comes from a job-board marketing page. Unverified, so do not rely on it. — [Gocareer](https://app.gocareer.club/it-jobs)

### Inferences
- Humain, STC, Elm, Aramco Digital, NEOM, Ooredoo, QNB and the consultancies are the likely target employers, but I did not retrieve evidence confirming current expat hiring at any of them (the names came from the brief).
- The Saudi regional HQ programme likely favours multinational offices hiring experienced expats, but I found no source on it.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on named-employer hiring, Qatar/Oman/Bahrain/Kuwait digital strategies, or role-specific (.NET, TPM) demand.

## 2. Pay and packages (SAR, QAR, KWD, OMR, BHD)

### Takeaway
There are no authoritative 2026 senior-tech pay tables for the five countries in my results. Saudi cloud-architect estimates cluster around SAR 300k to 400k a year (about USD 80k to 105k) with senior and consultancy roles higher, and the other countries have only thin data.

### Cited Findings
- Saudi cloud architect, annual: Payscale average base about SAR 350,000 (cloud-skills variant SAR 400,000; 10th to 90th percentile SAR 30k to 777k, low end looks like an outlier). — [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/SA/Job=Cloud_Solutions_Architect/Salary)
- Lifeindexed: SAR 300,500 a year gross (claims GASTAT basis, not verified). — [Lifeindexed](https://lifeindexed.com/salary/middle-east/saudi-arabia/cloud-architect/)
- GlobalCostData: median about USD 76,930, 25th to 75th percentile about USD 57.7k to 100k. The site says it is estimated from World Bank GNI, not verified labour data, so it is low quality. — [GlobalCostData](https://globalcostdata.com/salary/saudi-arabia/cloud-architect/)
- Zerotaxjobs: EY Riyadh intermediate cloud architect/engineer about USD 125k (range USD 100k to 150k). Possibly crowd-sourced; unverified. — [Zerotaxjobs](https://zerotaxjobs.com/salaries/companies/ernst-young/cloud-architectengineer-technology-consulting-clou-tqhopxko)
- Jooble reports SAR 35,000 a month but is internally inconsistent with its own annual maximum (SAR 420k). — [Jooble](https://sa.jooble.org/salary/cloud-architect)
- Qatar software engineer 2026 average QAR 280,300 a year (about QAR 23,358 a month), reportedly from PSA Qatar labour data, gross, no income tax. Aggregator; not senior-specific. — [Lifeindexed Qatar](https://lifeindexed.com/salary/middle-east/qatar/software-engineer/)
- Kuwait: a 2024 guide estimated mid-career white-collar packages around KWD 1,200 a month (Michael Page/Payscale cited). Another overview says Oman and Kuwait expat pay is roughly 10 to 30% below Saudi/UAE with lower cost of living. Both low-authority and not tech-specific. — [Koha ratings guide](https://ratings.koha-community.org/en/normal-salary-in-kuwait.html); [Expat Arrivals](https://www.expatarrivals.com/a-comparison-of-expat-salaries-in-the-middle-east)
- Bahrain tech pay quoted at USD 3,200 to 7,000 a month, zero income tax (low-authority recruiter blog). — [Huntlo](https://www.huntlo.ai/blog/gulf-hiring-trends-2026-what-recruiters-need-to-know)
- Pay-rise forecast for 2026 (survey published 2025): Saudi 4.6%, Qatar and Oman 4.3%. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2025/jobs/jobs-paychecks-uae-salary-rise-2026-forecast/)
- Conflict: one search summary reported an Oman figure in QAR, and a Bahrain "BHD 24,900" figure looked like a currency mismatch. I discarded both as unreliable.

### Inferences
- If Saudi architect pay is SAR 300k to 400k, that is about USD 80k to 107k (SAR is pegged at 3.75 per USD; this arithmetic is mine). Against EUR 72k to 100k gross in Europe, tax-free Gulf pay is competitive only at the upper end once Europe's income tax and social contributions are deducted. This comparison is my reasoning and I found no sourced comparison.
- Qatar and Saudi are likely the highest-paying of the five and Bahrain/Oman/Kuwait lower, but the evidence is weak.

### Gaps
- No sourced figures for relocation components (flights, housing allowance, schooling, medical) or end-of-service gratuity rules per country.
- No 2026 Hays, Michael Page, Cooper Fitch or foundit tech salary tables retrieved. Hays and Michael Page PDFs are at hays.ae and michaelpage.ae and should be pulled directly.
- No KWD, OMR or BHD senior-tech figures of acceptable quality; no current exchange-rate-checked EUR conversions.

## 3. Visa mechanics, Pakistani-national restrictions, localisation

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia is the main risk: reports describe temporary work-visa suspensions affecting Pakistan in 2025 and Feb 2026, but sources conflict on scope and current status. Kuwait reportedly reopened in May 2025. I found nothing for Bahrain, Qatar or Oman.

### Cited Findings
- Fragomen (via search snippet; page returned 403) and a law firm report that Saudi authorities stopped accepting, processing and issuing Temporary Work Visas for nationals of 17 countries including Pakistan (reported as Feb 2026). No formal public announcement or duration has been confirmed. — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/saudi-arabia-temporary-suspension-of-temporary-work-visas-for-select-nationals.html); [T&S Law](https://tandslaw.com/saudi-arabia-temporary-suspension-of-temporary-work-visas-for-select-nationalities/)
- May 2025: Saudi paused issuing block work-visa quotas for 14 countries including Pakistan, around Hajj, with possible relaxation afterwards. — [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/30-May-2025/saudi-arabia-halts-work-visas-for-14-countries-including-pakistan-heres-why)
- Contradicting source: Jobbatical (updated 1 Sep 2026) says there is no nationality-based ban on Saudi work visas; Pakistan is "high-volume, quota-based" with possible delays or temporary quota pauses, and HR should plan 4 to 12 extra weeks. Hajj-related visit/Umrah/family/business visa suspensions for Pakistan were expected to run to mid-June 2026 and are described as temporary. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/saudi-visa-nationality-restrictions)
- Kuwait lifted its ban and resumed work visas for Pakistanis in May 2025 (a manpower-agency guide, low authority); a candidate on a visit visa must exit and re-enter on an employer-sponsored work visa. — [Iqbal Manpower](https://iqbalmanpower.com/kuwait-work-visa-for-pakistani-workers-2026-guide/)
- Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE): foreign engineers must register for iqama issue or renewal. The SCE has reported 30,000+ fake engineering degrees, so degree verification is strict. Professional registration for non-engineer iqama professions costs SAR 1,250 for 3 years (SCE page). A reported pre-arrival professional test for expat engineers was a discussion, not a confirmed rule. Relevance to a software engineer depends on the iqama job title and is not established. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/saudi-arabia/30000-fake-engineers-netted-510011); [SCE](https://www.saudieng.sa/English/Eservices/Accreditation); [Ajel](https://english.ajel.sa/news/expat-engineers-need-to-clear-a-test-to-enter-saudi-arabia)
- Nitaqat: a new three-year cycle (reported 2026 to 2028, launched between Nov 2025 and Apr 2026), 269+ professions with quotas, engineering reportedly 30% Saudi for firms with 5+ engineers. Secondary sources that do not fully agree; no IT-specific quota found. — [Nimbus](https://nimbusconsultancy.com/ksa/company-formation-in-the-ksa-2026-business-setup-saudi-arabia/); [Y-Axis](https://www.y-axis.com/job-outlook/saudi-arabia/)
- Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain have expanded nationalisation quotas (Kuwait: 75%+ banking, 65%+ telecom; Oman: rising in IT). Bayt employer blog, 2025. — [Bayt](https://www.bayt.com/en/employers/blog/32253/nationalization-in-kuwait-oman-and-bahrain-what-employers-need-to-know-in-2025/)
- Unrelated to Gulf work visas but relevant to travel: Pakistan suspended visa on arrival and free visa prior to arrival for 126 countries from 1 Jan 2026 (low-authority). — [VisasUpdate](https://www.visasupdate.com/post/pakistan-suspends-visa-on-arrival-visa-prior-to-arrival-126-countries-2026-evisa-mandatory)

### Inferences
- Because skilled employer-sponsored hires may be treated differently from block/temporary work visas, the real impact on a senior engineer is uncertain. A named employer's HR or immigration counsel should confirm current status before any resignation or commitment.
- Degree attestation (HEC, Pakistan MOFA, destination embassy) is standard practice but I did not retrieve a source. Treat as unverified.

### Gaps
- No primary-source confirmation of current Saudi suspension status as of Nov 2026.
- Nothing found on Qatar, Oman or Bahrain visa processing for Pakistanis, kafala reform details, or Saudi Qiwa/Musaned mechanics.
