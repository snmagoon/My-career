# Western Europe work-permit routes, salary thresholds and tech hiring (9 countries) for a non-EU senior .NET/Azure/AI engineer, as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-03. Almost all sources are law-firm alerts, immigration consultancies or recruiter blogs, not official government pages. Official pages were not fetched (search snippets only), so every threshold should be re-checked on the official site before an application decision. Candidate: Pakistani national, BSc Computer Software Engineering (2017), about 8-9 years of experience, no local language.

## Q1. Main permit route, 2026 salary thresholds, shortage-list status, processing time, sponsor requirement, family/PR path, 2026 policy changes (per country)

### Takeaway
A BSc plus 8-9 years of experience qualifies for the EU Blue Card or the national skilled-worker equivalent in every country. EUR 72-100K clears every threshold below. The thresholds that bind are Luxembourg (EUR 65,652 Blue Card), France (EUR 59,373 Blue Card), Belgium (Flanders and Wallonia Blue Card, about EUR 63-69K) and Switzerland (quota). Ireland, the Netherlands and Germany have comparatively low floors. Verified data on processing times, family and PR paths, and sponsor-registration rules was mostly not retrieved (see Gaps).

### Cited Findings

#### Ireland (Critical Skills Employment Permit, CSEP)
- From 1 March 2026 the CSEP minimum is EUR 40,904 with a relevant degree (up from EUR 38,000, a 7.66% rise rather than the 15.8% originally planned). Without a relevant degree the figure is EUR 68,911 (up from EUR 64,000). Recent-graduate rate is EUR 36,848. — [Newland Chase](https://newlandchase.com/government-of-ireland-unveils-roadmap-for-gradual-increase-in-employment-permit-salary-thresholds/); [Vialto](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/ireland-immigration-changes-to-employment-permit-salary-thresholds-from-1-march-2026); official release at [enterprise.gov.ie](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/news-and-events/department-news/2025/december/20251202.html) (not opened)
- Thresholds for 2027 are shown as "TBD" in a DLA Piper table, so further increases are expected but not fixed. — [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/Upcoming-increases-in-remuneration-thresholds-for-employment-permits)
- The Labour Market Needs Test is waived for roles on the Critical Skills Occupations List. Secondary guides list software developers and engineers among the commonly used ICT roles at EUR 40,904+. A relevant degree is required. — [Expert Consultants](https://expertconsultants.ie/critical-skills-occupations-list/)
- The occupations lists were updated in May 2026 (four additions, including optometrists and IP professionals). Sources disagree on the effective date, 13 May vs 22 May 2026. — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/en-ie/insights/2026/05/27/irelands-employment-permit-occupations-lists-whats-changed-in-may-2026)
- CSEP is described as the fastest route to Stamp 4 residence. — [Expert Consultants](https://expertconsultants.ie/critical-skills-employment-permit/)

#### United Kingdom (Skilled Worker visa)
- General Skilled Worker minimum is GBP 41,700 a year, or the occupation's going rate if higher. Lower floor of GBP 33,400 applies to new entrants, relevant STEM PhDs and Immigration Salary List (ISL) roles. — [UK Visa Info](https://ukvisainfo.co.uk/blog/uk-skilled-worker-visa-salary-threshold-2026); [JPS Immigration](https://www.jpsimmigration.com/skilled-worker-visa-2026-new-salary-rules-and-what-they-mean-for-sponsors/)
- Source conflict: one source cites GBP 38,700 as the general figure (appears to be the pre-July-2025 number); sources also differ on whether the ISL gives a 20% discount or just the GBP 33,400 floor with the full going rate. — [JPS Immigration](https://www.jpsimmigration.com/skilled-worker-visa-2026-new-salary-rules-and-what-they-mean-for-sponsors/)
- The ISL / Temporary Shortage List is time-limited. Most entries are due to be removed on 31 December 2026, subject to review. Software-developer ISL status was not confirmed. — [Bird & Bird](https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/uk/uk-immigration-law-key-changes-in-2025-and-what-to-expect-in-2026)
- Settlement: the proposed "earned settlement" model sets a 10-year baseline for standard Skilled Workers (RQF 6+). It is not yet in force; final rules were expected around autumn 2026 and might apply retrospectively. The English requirement for settlement rises from B1 to B2 for applications from 26 March 2027. Since 8 April 2026 sponsors must pay the full required salary in every pay period. — [Magrath mid-year review](https://www.magrath.co.uk/resources/news/uk-immigration-mid-year-review-2026-for-businesses/); [Pennington's](https://www.penningtonslaw.com/insights/earned-settlement-consultation-closes-where-we-stand/); [Vialto, 5 March 2026 statement of changes](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/united-kingdom-immigration-new-statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-published-5-march-2026)
- Employer must hold a sponsor licence (inherent to the route; the search results treated it as given and no source I opened states it explicitly).

#### Germany (EU Blue Card, IT-specialist rules)
- 2026 Blue Card minimum is EUR 50,700 gross a year; the reduced threshold is EUR 45,934.20 (shortage/MINT occupations, IT specialists, recent graduates). — [RT Partner](https://www.rtpartner.de/en/immigration/blaue-karte-eu-mindestgehalt-2026/); [Aldag Legal](https://aldaglegal.com/eu-blue-card-germany-2026/)
- Conflict on Federal Employment Agency approval: one source says it is generally not required at the EUR 50,700 level; another says it is required under the reduced threshold. — [Aldag Legal](https://aldaglegal.com/eu-blue-card-germany-2026/)
- IT specialists without a degree may qualify with 3+ years of IT experience in the last 7 years (not relevant here since the candidate has a BSc; not confirmed against an official source). — [MS in Germany](https://msingermany.com/blog/blue-card-germany-it-professionals)

#### Netherlands (Highly Skilled Migrant / kennismigrant)
- 2026 gross monthly minimum (excluding 8% holiday allowance): EUR 5,942 for age 30+, EUR 4,357 for under 30, EUR 3,122 for orientation-year graduates. The candidate is 30+, so the figure is EUR 5,942 a month (about EUR 71,300 a year before holiday pay). — [Grant Thornton NL](https://www.grantthornton.nl/insights/human-capital-services/wijzigingen-salarisdrempels-2026/); [Visa Atlas](https://visaatlas.org/policy-updates/nl-2026-01-kennismigrant-salary-thresholds)
- Source conflict: one blog gives EUR 4,754 as the reduced graduate figure; treated as an error. Some pages still show the 2025 figures (EUR 5,688 / 4,171 / 2,989). — [Briefly](https://briefly.co.za/people/255834-how-2026-dutch-skilled-migrant-salary-hikes-impact-south-african-applicants/)
- Bonuses and overtime do not count toward the threshold; only guaranteed base pay. — same sources
- Deloitte NL published 2026 kennismigrant and Blue Card thresholds (content not retrieved). — [Deloitte NL](https://www.deloitte.com/nl/en/services/tax/perspectives/highly-skilled-migrant-and-european-blue-card-2026-salary-thresholds)
- The route requires a recognised sponsor employer (known from the IND system; the retrieved results did not state it explicitly).

#### Belgium (single permit, highly qualified worker; regional)
- 2026 highly qualified thresholds: Brussels about EUR 44,441 a year (78% of EUR 4,748 monthly average, i.e. EUR 3,703.44 a month; unchanged from 2025), Flanders EUR 48,912 (2025 figure retained), Wallonia EUR 53,220 (indexed 1 Jan 2026 from EUR 51,613). — [Brussels Times](https://brusselstimes.com/belgium/1929259/foreign-workers-in-belgium-face-steep-hike-in-minimum-salary-requirements/); [EY Wallonia alert](https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0271-belgiums-walloon-region-increases-salary-thresholds-for-work-permits-and-single-permits-effective-1-january-2026); [EP Advisory](https://ep-advisory.com/blog/how-to-find-a-job-in-belgium-brussels-and-belgium-job-search-guide/)
- Conflict: Borderpilot gives EUR 51,551+ for Brussels, which does not match 12 x EUR 3,703.44; treated as an error. — [Borderpilot](https://borderpilot.com/blog/from-bengaluru-to-brussels-an-indian-developers-guide-to-belgium)
- Belgian EU Blue Card thresholds: Flanders EUR 63,586, Wallonia EUR 68,815, Brussels EUR 56,976. — [Brussels Times](https://brusselstimes.com/belgium/1929259/foreign-workers-in-belgium-face-steep-hike-in-minimum-salary-requirements/)
- A larger Brussels adjustment may come in 2027 (speculative). Flanders adds a EUR 180 regional fee from 1 Sept 2026, paid by the employer. — same source
- Ordinary highly qualified-worker thresholds are low relative to the EUR 72-100K target; the Blue Card is mainly relevant for EU mobility.

#### Luxembourg (EU Blue Card)
- Blue Card minimum is EUR 65,652 gross a year (about EUR 5,471 a month) for applications from 3 March 2026 (ministerial regulation of 23 Feb 2026, published 27 Feb). Previous figure was EUR 63,408. No reduced threshold for shortage occupations since June 2024. — [Arendt](https://www.arendt.com/news-insights/news/eu-blue-card-increase-of-minimum-salary-threshold-2/); [Vialto](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/luxembourg-immigration-increase-of-salary-threshold-for-eu-blue-card); [LuxToday](https://luxtoday.lu/en/luxembourg-en/luxembourg-tightens-conditions-for-obtaining-the-eu-blue-card-salary-threshold-increased-to-63408)
- Conflict: Fragomen's snippet shows EUR 71,946 (up from EUR 69,858), and a comparison site shows EUR 80,640; neither matches the law-firm figures and may refer to older data or another measure. — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/minimum-salary-increased-for-blue-card-program.html)

#### Austria (Red-White-Red Card / Blue Card)
- 2026 Blue Card minimum is EUR 55,678 gross a year (raised 1 Jan 2026). Other key workers: EUR 3,465 a month. Highly qualified under the Red-White-Red Card: EUR 7,740 a month per an EY alert. — [Vialto](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/austria-immigration-immigration-2026-update); [EY](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/technical/tax-alerts/documents/austria-increases-minimum-salary-requirements-for-foreign-employees.pdf)
- Shortage occupation route: sources conflict. Workinaustria says no general minimum salary; another says collective-agreement pay applies; Global Law Experts mentions a reduced 1.2x median floor (unconfirmed). Vialto notes a reduced 2026 shortage list; IT status not confirmed. — [Work in Austria](https://www.workinaustria.com/en/blog/news-on-the-red-white-red-card-shortage-occupations); [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/immigration-lawyers-austria/)

#### Switzerland (B permit, third-country quota)
- 2026 quotas for third-country nationals unchanged: 4,500 B permits and 4,000 L permits (confirmed by the Federal Council 19 Nov 2025, ordinance effective 1 Jan 2026). Renewals of B permits are outside the quota. — [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-2364-switzerland-to-retain-work-permit-quotas-for-eea-uk-and-third-country-nationals-in-2026); [Clark Hill](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/switzerland-work-permit-quota-allocations-for-2026/); [Vischer](https://www.vischer.com/en/knowledge/blog/quotas-for-foreigners-in-switzerland-in-2026/)
- Quota utilisation is disputed: about 74% by end-2024 (Vischer) vs about 52% by end-Sept 2025 (Migaku, blog). A Fragomen article dated 14 Sept 2026 asks whether quotas will run out before year end; no usage data was retrievable. — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/swiss-immigration-quotas-2026-are-work-permit-quotas-likely-to-run-out-before-year-end.html)
- UK nationals have a separate 3,500 pool, irrelevant for this candidate.

#### France (Passeport Talent salarié qualifié / Blue Card)
- Passeport Talent skilled employee reference salary EUR 39,582 gross a year; EU Blue Card threshold EUR 59,373 (1.5x), per the 29 Aug 2025 ministerial order. — [Integration France](https://www.integrationfrance.fr/articles/passeport-talent-salarie-qualifie-2026-salaire-conditions); [Jobbatical](https://jobbatical.com/blog/france-talent-passport-salary-threshold)
- Conflict: Jobbatical gives about EUR 47,498 for STEM/ICT shortage roles (unverified against official text); an older Blue Card figure of about EUR 53,836 is a 2025 number. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/france-salary-threshold-eu-blue-card)

### Inferences
- With a BSc and 8-9 years, the candidate clears degree/experience conditions everywhere; the salary floor is the binding filter only in Luxembourg, France and Belgium (Blue Card); an offer of at least EUR 72K clears all of them.
- Cheapest routes by salary floor: Ireland (EUR 40,904), Germany (EUR 45,934-50,700), Brussels (about EUR 44K). Netherlands is about EUR 71K a year for age 30+, so it is close to the bottom of the target band.
- The Swiss quota is a small, hard cap and Swiss employers must usually show no suitable EU/EEA candidate (background knowledge, not retrieved), so Switzerland is the hardest for a non-EU candidate with no local language.
- UK settlement is now a 10-year baseline proposal and the ISL concession is ending, making the UK weaker for long-term residence than Ireland or Germany/Netherlands (Blue Card/knowledge-migrant routes with settled PR typically available in about 5 years or less, background knowledge, not verified here).

### Gaps
- Official-site confirmation (gov.uk, enterprise.gov.ie, IND, make-it-in-germany) was not obtained for any figure.
- Processing times per country were not retrieved.
- Family reunification and PR timelines were not retrieved for any country (Ireland Stamp 4 family rules, Germany 21-27 months PR with B1 German, Dutch 5-year PR, Luxembourg 5-year residence).
- UK software developer SOC going rate and ISL status; whether the Temporary Shortage List is extended beyond 31 Dec 2026.
- Whether Ireland's CSEP still treats the candidate's BSc as a "relevant degree" is likely yes (IT) but was not checked against the official list.
- Austria: whether IT roles are on the 2026 shortage list; Belgium Blue Card vs single-permit choice for the candidate.
- Swiss permit conditions (priority for residents, salary expectations, employer proof).
- Sponsor-registration rules (UK licence, Dutch recognised sponsor, Irish none) were not confirmed from official text.

## Q2. Tech hiring climate in 2026 and realistic senior salaries (is EUR 72-100K realistic?)

### Takeaway
Salary benchmarks suggest EUR 72-100K is realistic for a senior engineer in Ireland, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany (big cities) and Switzerland (well above), borderline for Belgium and Austria (mostly below EUR 87-98K at the top), and harder in France. I found no reliable 2026 layoff or .NET/Azure-specific demand data; hiring-climate claims are weak.

### Cited Findings
- Netherlands senior software engineer average about EUR 73,771 (range EUR 55-110K, Payscale 2026); LifeIndexed all-engineer average EUR 72,600. — [Payscale NL](https://www.payscale.com/research/NL/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary); [LifeIndexed NL](https://lifeindexed.com/salary/europe/netherlands/software-engineer/2026/)
- Netherlands senior .NET developer with Azure average EUR 61,424 (Payscale, small sample). — [Payscale NL .NET](https://www.payscale.com/research/NL/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary/8830774c/Amsterdam-Microsoft-Azure)
- Germany senior pay EUR 72,000-95,250 (Glassdoor-derived, June 2026); typical software engineer range EUR 45-110K+. — [InedJobs](https://www.inedjobs.com/2026/06/software-engineer-salary-germany-2026.html)
- Ireland: Levels.fyi-based median total compensation EUR 101,127 vs EUR 89,987 Netherlands and EUR 81,495 Germany (total comp, including stock). Remote-pay guide range EUR 70-105K. — [Ravio](https://ravio.com/blog/software-engineer-salary-trends); [JobRise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/remote-software-engineer-salary-europe/)
- Luxembourg senior average about EUR 69,209 (range EUR 49-107K), experienced tier about EUR 78,520; all-engineer average EUR 93,500 (LifeIndexed) and a Jobicy median of about EUR 114,500 (outlier). — [Payscale LU](https://www.payscale.com/research/LU/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary); [LifeIndexed BE](https://lifeindexed.com/salary/europe/belgium/software-engineer/); [Jobicy](https://jobicy.com/salaries/lu/software-developer.md)
- Belgium (Brussels) senior average about EUR 69,057 (range EUR 41-98K). Austria senior about EUR 68,494 (range EUR 56-87K). — [Payscale BE](https://www.payscale.com/research/BE/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary/f5304378/Brussels); [Payscale AT](https://www.payscale.com/research/AT/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary)
- France: all-engineer average EUR 62,800 (LifeIndexed); about EUR 52K for 5 years' experience (Salaire Clair, rough). — [LifeIndexed](https://lifeindexed.com/salary/europe/belgium/software-engineer/); [Salaire Clair](https://salaireclair.fr/blog/salaire-ingenieur-luxembourg-2026)
- Switzerland (Zurich) senior: Payscale median about CHF 126K (experienced, 25th-75th CHF 103-134K); Levels.fyi median total comp CHF 129K; JobRise CHF 130-180K. — [Payscale Zurich](https://www.payscale.com/research/CH/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary/41d3d495/Zurich); [Levels.fyi Zurich](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/levels/senior/locations/zurich-che); [JobRise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/senior-software-engineer-salary-switzerland-2026/)
- AI/MLOps specialists are paid a premium over generalist seniors in Germany (about USD 60-74/h vs 45-53/h); a claim of ML engineer roles +59% since 2020 and general SWE openings -49% is an unverified secondary summary. — [Ravio / market summaries](https://ravio.com/blog/software-engineer-salary-trends)
- UK salary data and English-language job prevalence, relocation package norms, layoffs/freezes: no usable sources found.

### Inferences
- Source methods conflict (base vs total comp, self-reported vs posted); Payscale tends to read lower and Levels.fyi higher because of multinational/big-tech skew. The target band EUR 72-100K is in the middle of Dutch, German and Irish senior base ranges but sits at or above the top end for Belgium and Austria and for most French employers.
- The senior .NET/Azure niche appears to pay at or below generalist senior averages in the Netherlands (Payscale sample), so an AI/architect/tech-lead profile is the stronger pitch for the upper half of the band.
- English-language prevalence is likely highest in Ireland, Netherlands, Luxembourg and Brussels, and lowest in France and Austria (background knowledge, not verified).

### Gaps
- No 2026 layoff or hiring-freeze data by country, no .NET/C#/Azure job-count data, no TPM/architect-specific pay.
- No UK, Irish or French senior base-pay figures from a robust survey (Hays, Michael Page, Robert Walters guides not retrieved).
- Relocation package norms not found.
- English-language job prevalence not quantified.

## Q3. Which cities and employer types sponsor most

### Takeaway
I did not retrieve reliable data on which employers sponsor most. Only directional hints exist, and recruiter-style lists were low quality.

### Cited Findings
- Ireland CSEP: the listed-occupation route exempts the labour market test, which favours multinationals and consultancies in Dublin (inference from the LMNT waiver). — [Expert Consultants](https://expertconsultants.ie/critical-skills-occupations-list/)
- Germany: an IT-focused Blue Card guide lists top hiring cities; the content was not retrieved. — [MS in Germany](https://msingermany.com/blog/blue-card-germany-it-professionals)
- Zurich is where large US tech employers pay highest (e.g. Google senior base reportedly CHF 195-235K, JobRise blog, low rigor). — [JobRise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/google-zurich-engineer-salary-2026/)

### Inferences
- Likely best sponsor targets by country (background knowledge, not verified): Dublin (Microsoft, Google, Meta, banks, Accenture), Amsterdam/Eindhoven (recognised-sponsor multinationals, consultancies), Berlin/Munich/Frankfurt/Hamburg, London (UK licence holders), Luxembourg City (banks and fund administration), Brussels (EU-institution vendors and consultancies). Verify with the official recognised-sponsor and licence registers.

### Gaps
- No sponsor-volume statistics (UK Home Office sponsor register, IND recognised sponsors, Irish permit statistics by employer).
- No country-level evidence on consultancies vs banks vs multinationals for sponsorship.
- Cities for Austria, France and Switzerland not researched.
