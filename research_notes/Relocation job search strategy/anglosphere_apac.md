# Work-permit routes and tech hiring: Canada, Australia, NZ, Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong (Pakistani senior .NET/Azure/AI engineer, employer sponsorship, as of Nov 2026)

Method note: about 16 searches/fetches. Only 3 pages were fetched in full (Baker McKenzie Malaysia, Baker McKenzie Australia, INZ NZ). Everything else rests on search-result summaries of mostly consultancy and aggregator pages. No official IRCC, Home Affairs, MOM or HK ImmD page was opened. No Hays, Robert Walters, Michael Page or Seek salary-guide figures were reached. Treat everything as provisional. Verify on official sites before acting.

FX assumption (mine, NOT sourced, verify): EUR 72K-100K is roughly AUD 125-175K, CAD 115-160K, NZD 135-190K, SGD 105-145K, MYR 350-490K, HKD 650-900K. This assumes about EUR 1 = AUD 1.75, CAD 1.6, NZD 1.9, SGD 1.45, MYR 4.9, HKD 9.0. Re-check against live rates.

## Canada: routes, thresholds, 2025-26 changes

### Takeaway
The employer-sponsored route is LMIA via the Global Talent Stream (GTS, about 2-week target), with PR via Express Entry category draws. The 2026-28 Levels Plan cuts temporary-resident intake sharply, so work-permit supply is tighter. The GTS rules look unchanged, but I found no official source confirming that.

### Cited Findings
- No official ESDC announcement of GTS rule changes for 2026 turned up. The structure is still Category A (designated-partner referral) and Category B (in-demand occupations list). — [Search summary of consultancy pages, e.g. Sobirovs GTS](https://sobirovs.com/employers/global-talent-stream-canada/)
- GTS processing times conflict across sources. One reports it exceeding the 10-day standard in March 2026. Another summarises ESDC August 2026 data as meeting the standard. A June 2026 summary reports 9 business days. — [Liberty Immigration, Mar 2026](https://libertyimmigration.ca/lmia-processing-times-2026-wait-times-rise-for-high-wage-and-low-wage-streams/); [Liberty, Aug 2026](https://libertyimmigration.ca/?p=12383)
- Quebec cut its fast-track LMIA occupations from 76 to 60 (transition to 26 Mar 2026). One source says April 2026 ESDC rules changed the low-wage stream. This is not relevant to a senior tech role. — [CanX Global](https://canxglobal.com/quebec-lmia-simplified-list-2026/)
- The 2026-28 Levels Plan reportedly has PR at 380,000 for 2026 (flat), with Federal High Skilled at 109,000 (some sources say 115,000, unreconciled). Temporary-resident arrivals are 385,000 in 2026 and 370,000 in 2027-28. The new worker-arrival target is about 230,000 (a 37% drop). The International Mobility Program is 170,000. — [UP Immigration](https://www.upimmigration.ca/blog/canada-immigration-levels-plan-2026-2028-targets); [Immigration2Canada](https://www.immigration2canada.com/canadas-2026-2028-immigration-levels-plan-big-cuts-to-temporary-residents/amp/)
- Express Entry uses category-based draws. Sources say tech or STEM is a priority category, but I did not find the official 2026 category list. — [CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/?p=61056)

### Inferences
- A GTS LMIA is feasible for a senior .NET/Azure/AI role, since software engineers are on the GTS Category B list as I understand it (unverified). A direct overseas hire is possible but employers must be willing to run an LMIA. Provincial programs (e.g. OINP, BC PNP Tech) were not researched.
- Pakistani citizens have no specific restriction found, but I did not check for one.

### Gaps
- Official IRCC and ESDC pages were not read. The current GTS wage and occupation requirements, the 2026 Express Entry category list and CRS cut-offs, and provincial programs (BC Tech, OINP, Alberta) are unconfirmed.
- No data on Pakistan-specific processing issues such as police certificates or biometrics.

## Australia: routes, thresholds, 2026 changes

### Takeaway
The route is the Skills in Demand (SID) 482 visa. Core Skills requires an income of at least AUD 79,499, and the Specialist Skills pathway at least AUD 146,717, from 1 July 2026. A typical senior .NET salary falls below the Specialist threshold, so Core Skills applies unless the role is high-paid. Core Skills needs an occupation on the Core Skills Occupation List and a market-rate salary.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 July 2026 the CSIT rises from AUD 76,515 to AUD 79,499 and the SSIT from AUD 141,210 to AUD 146,717 (a 3.8% increase). These apply to nominations lodged on or after 1 July 2026. Subclass 494 and 186 use the CSIT. Salary must also meet market rate. — [Baker McKenzie, Mar 2026](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/03/australia-skilled-visa-income-threshold-indexation-2026-27) (fetched)
- Conflict: other sources give SSIT as AUD 146,576 and CSIT as AUD 79,423. These are likely inaccurate. Baker McKenzie is the better source. — [Parish Patience / search summary](https://www.parishpatience.com.au/482-visa-changes-2026-whats-different/)
- Specialist Skills is described as for senior or highly specialised workers, with a visa of up to four years. — [Visa Envoy](https://visaenvoy.com/skills-in-demand-visa/specialist-skills-pathway/)

### Inferences
- A Pakistani applicant with AUD 130-170K offers would sit between the CSIT and SSIT. The route is Core Skills if the nominated occupation (e.g. Software Engineer / Developer Programmer) is on the Core Skills list (not verified). Specialist Skills may need the top of the range.
- Skills assessment requirements and the Core Skills Occupation List were not checked.

### Gaps
- Home Affairs pages were not read. The Core Skills Occupation List contents, skills assessment, experience (usually 1 year) and visa fees are unconfirmed.
- Whether Pakistan-specific health or character checks add delay is unknown.

## New Zealand: AEWV, median wage, Green List

### Takeaway
The AEWV needs an accredited employer and a job on the National Occupation List. The immigration median wage rose to NZD 35.00/hr on 9 March 2026, but the general median-wage requirement for AEWV was removed in March 2025. Sources conflict on how it applies to ICT, so check INZ directly.

### Cited Findings
- From 9 March 2026 the median wage is NZD 35.00/hr (was 33.56), based on June 2025 data. The AEWV general median-wage requirement was removed in March 2025. Settings still tied to it include Green List pay, an exemption from advertising and minimum skills for roles paid at least 2x the median, and partner and dependant income thresholds. — [INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/new-occupations-recognised-under-the-national-occupation-list-and-annual-median-wage-increase) (fetched)
- The Green List threshold for partners rises from NZD 33.56 to 35.00/hr. — same INZ page
- 47 occupations were added to the National Occupation List (skill levels 1-3). The INZ page does not say which, and no ICT roles are confirmed. — same INZ page
- Adult minimum wage is NZD 23.95/hr from 1 April 2026. — [search summary, Kiwifern / news sources](https://www.kiwifern.com/work-visa/)
- Older ICT pay rules (Aug 2025, 2.08x median = NZD 69.80/hr for ICT, 3.12x for contract-for-services ICT) were found in a search summary. Whether these still apply is unclear and conflicts with the statement that the median requirement was removed. — [Envoy Global](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/new-zealand-introduces-changes-to-aewv-form-and-wage-thresholds/)

### Inferences
- NZD 35/hr is about NZD 73K/yr full time, well below a senior salary, so thresholds are unlikely to bind. The binding constraints are employer accreditation, job advertising (waived at 2x the median, about NZD 70/hr, or about NZD 145K/yr) and local demand.

### Gaps
- The skill-level classification of software roles on the National Occupation List and the path from AEWV to residence (Skilled Migrant Category points) were not verified.
- The NZ tech hiring climate was not assessed beyond salary aggregators. No recruiter guide was found.

## Singapore: Employment Pass, COMPASS, 2026 salary

### Takeaway
The EP qualifying salary is SGD 5,600/month (non-financial) since 1 Jan 2025, rising with age to about SGD 10,700 at 45+. COMPASS requires 40 points. A senior engineer at SGD 10-14K/month clears the base, but COMPASS is the main hurdle. The minimum rises to SGD 6,000 on 1 Jan 2027.

### Cited Findings
- EP minimum SGD 5,600/month (non-financial), SGD 6,200 for financial services, scaling to SGD 10,700 (SGD 11,800 financial) at age 45+. COMPASS needs 40 points; salary above SGD 22,500 is reported to exempt. 2027 increase to SGD 6,000 (SGD 6,600 financial) from 1 Jan 2027. — [Asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/employer-of-record-singapore/employment-pass-singapore/); [3ECPA](https://www.3ecpa.com.sg/blog/singapore-employment-pass-requirements-how-to-apply/) (third-party guides, not MOM)
- An EOR cannot sponsor an EP (Asanify title). — [Asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/employer-of-record-singapore/employment-pass-singapore/)
- Senior .NET salary: Secondtalent rate card SGD 10,500-14,000/month; Payscale shows lower, about SGD 59K/yr median for experienced, which looks low and probably reflects thin or self-reported data. — [Secondtalent](https://www.secondtalent.com/developer-rate-card/dotnet-developer-singapore/); [Payscale SG](https://www.payscale.com/research/SG/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary)

### Inferences
- SGD 10.5-14K/month is about SGD 126-168K/yr, roughly EUR 87-116K at my assumed rate. This overlaps the target range. COMPASS points (salary vs peers, qualifications, diversity, local-hiring, skills shortage) were not researched. Many Singapore employers prefer local interviews but overseas hiring for senior tech is common (not sourced).

### Gaps
- MOM pages not read. COMPASS point breakdown, the Shortage Occupation List, Pakistan-specific scrutiny and the education-institution tiering are unconfirmed.

## Malaysia: Employment Pass, 2026 changes

### Takeaway
From 1 June 2026, MDEC-registered company EP minimums doubled: Cat I RM 20,000+, Cat II RM 10,000-19,999, Cat III RM 5,000-9,999. A senior .NET/Azure salary of about RM 15-25K/month would sit in Cat I or II. No Pakistan-specific ban was found, but nationality-specific checks may exist.

### Cited Findings
- Effective 1 June 2026 (new and renewal applications): Cat I from RM 10,000 to RM 20,000; Cat II from RM 5,000-9,999 to RM 10,000-19,999; Cat III from RM 3,000-4,999 to RM 5,000-9,999. Applies to MDEC-registered companies; no similar increase had been announced for non-MDEC (ESD) applications at the time. No grandfathering mentioned. — [Baker McKenzie, Jan 2026](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/01/malaysia-increase-in-employment-pass-salary-requirements) (fetched)
- Other reports: Category II capped at 10 years with a succession plan; Cat III holders can bring dependants from 1 June 2026; only basic salary counts. Sources disagree on pass duration and on the Cat III figure. — [EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/malaysia-revises-minimum-salary-for-employment-pass-applications-effective-june-1-2026/); [People Matters](https://sea.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/recruitment/malaysia-to-double-expat-pass-salary-requirements-from-june-1-extends-work-permits-to-10-years-48048)
- Reform applies to all nationalities; no rule barring Pakistanis found. One guide says a MADANI tech-talent fast track for STEM roles exists (unverified). Pakistan appears on some country lists (e.g. medical exam for construction, manufacturing, mining). — [Where To Emigrate](https://wheretoemigrate.io/lanes/pakistan-to-malaysia-work-permit-2026); [BAL](https://www.bal.com/immigration-news/new-rules-impact-employment-passes-professional-visit-passes/) (low-quality / partial)
- Senior .NET pay: Payscale average about RM 114K/yr (10th-90th about RM 72-161K). — [Payscale MY](https://www.payscale.com/research/MY/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary)

### Inferences
- Local pay (RM 114K/yr is about EUR 23K) is far below EUR 72-100K. Reaching it needs a regional or multinational role at RM 350K+/yr. Realistic mainly through foreign-company or GCC roles; local-market roles will not match.
- With the RM 20K floor for Cat I, Cat II (RM 10-19.9K) is realistic for many local-market offers, but that is only about EUR 25-48K/yr.

### Gaps
- Official ESD / MyXpats / Immigration Department pages were not read. Conflicting reports on duration caps. No Pakistan-specific restriction confirmed or ruled out.

## Hong Kong: GEP, ASMTP, Pakistani nationals

### Takeaway
The GEP has no published minimum salary or quota, but pay must be at market level and the job offer must be genuine. Pakistan is not on the list of excluded nationalities in one undated FAQ, but I could not confirm Pakistan-specific treatment from official sources.

### Cited Findings
- GEP needs a confirmed job offer and relevant qualifications; no minimum salary or quota, but market-rate testing applies. Government fee HKD 1,900, normal processing about 4 weeks. SCMP found nearly half of successful applicants earned under HKD 20,000/month. — [Playroll](https://playroll.com/blog/general-employment-policy-requirements); [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/hong-kong-economy/article/3365664/visa-policy-brings-more-foreigners-short-term-jobs-and-hk20000-salaries)
- A FAQ lists GEP exclusions (mainland China, Afghanistan, Cuba, Laos, North Korea, Nepal, Vietnam) and Pakistan is absent; undated. — [Kaizen CPA](https://kaizencpa.com/Faq/info/id/483.html)
- Guidance reportedly updated by ImmD on 16 April 2026 on assessable-income evidence (not confirmed). — [search summary of third-party guide](https://visaatlas.org/visas/hong-kong/general-employment-policy)
- Senior .NET pay: Payscale average HKD 250,800 base (25th-75th HKD 110K-444K); C# page shows HKD 329,400, so Payscale itself conflicts. — [Payscale HK](https://www.payscale.com/research/HK/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary)

### Inferences
- HKD 250-330K is only about EUR 28-37K and looks too low for senior work. Real senior offers in finance/tech are probably much higher, but no good data was found. GEP is employer-led, so overseas hiring is possible, though Hong Kong employers often prefer local presence (not sourced).

### Gaps
- ImmD pages not read. ASMTP details (it covers mainland talent, so is not relevant to Pakistanis) and Top Talent Pass (income / university criteria) were not checked. Pakistani-specific vetting is not confirmed. No recruiter salary guide found.

## Tech hiring climate and salary vs EUR 72-100K

### Takeaway
Aggregator salary data suggest the EUR 72-100K range is realistic in Australia (senior AUD 130-170K), probably Canada (CAD 115-185K, upper end for Toronto / big cities), and Singapore (SGD 126-168K); borderline in NZ; unrealistic for typical local-market Malaysian and Hong Kong roles. This is based on weak sources.

### Cited Findings
- Australia: Payscale senior .NET with Azure AUD 128K (p25), 157K (median), 170K (p75); senior engineer with Azure average AUD 123,757. WhatIsTheSalary guide: Senior Software Engineer AUD 150-190K; Melbourne .NET/Azure/Angular AUD 120-155K. — [Payscale AU](https://www.payscale.com/research/AU/Job=Senior_.NET_Developer_%2F_Programmer/Salary/0ac47c8f/Experienced-Microsoft-Azure); [WhatIsTheSalary](https://whatisthesalary.com/guides/software-engineer-jobs-in-australia/)
- Canada: Indeed senior software engineer average CAD 135,174 (Jul 2026); Payscale senior developer average CAD 111,081 (about 87-140K); Motion Recruitment Toronto all-level average CAD 137,693; JobRise Ottawa CAD 125-180K, Calgary 125-185K. Robert Half says employers pay more for AI/ML skills. Michael Page (Mar 2026) says senior roles take longer to fill. — [Indeed CA](https://ca.indeed.com/career/senior-software-engineer/salaries); [Payscale CA](https://www.payscale.com/research/CA/Job=Senior_Software_Developer/Salary); [Michael Page](https://www.michaelpage.ca/recruitment-expertise/management-advice/canada-tech-hiring-2026); [Robert Half](https://www.roberthalf.com/ca/en/insights/research/it-and-tech-salary-guide-trends)
- NZ: Payscale senior software developer about NZD 119K (93-135K); Kiwifern says NZD 140-170K at established firms; Indeed senior developer NZD 140,714 (small sample); Glassdoor Intergen NZD 178K (18 salaries). — [Payscale NZ](https://www.payscale.com/research/NZ/Job=Senior_Software_Developer/Salary); [Kiwifern](https://www.kiwifern.com/posts/it-jobs-salary-new-zealand)
- Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong figures are listed in the sections above.

### Inferences
- Rough EUR equivalents (at my assumed rates): Australia 130-170K AUD is about EUR 74-97K; Canada 125-185K CAD is about EUR 78-115K (upper end needs major-city or AI-skilled roles); Singapore about EUR 87-116K; NZ 120-160K NZD is about EUR 63-84K; Malaysia and Hong Kong local-market figures found are well below target.
- Taxes and cost of living were not researched, so net comparison to EUR is not possible.
- AI skills may carry a premium (Robert Half, Canada), but I found no premium figures. TPM and architect roles were not separately priced.

### Gaps
- No relocation-package norms found for any country. No data on how often employers sponsor from overseas versus requiring local presence. No Hays, Robert Walters, Seek or Levels.fyi figures were reached. All pay data is self-reported aggregator data, not recruiter-verified.

## Ranking for a candidate applying from Pakistan (my judgment, low-medium confidence)

Suggested order of realism: 1) Singapore and Australia (clear sponsored routes, pay near target; Singapore COMPASS and Australia occupation / skills-assessment are the caveats), 2) Canada via GTS (pay fits, but employer LMIA effort and a tighter temporary-worker environment), 3) New Zealand (needs accredited employer; pay borderline), 4) Malaysia (easy-ish route but pay far below target unless a foreign-company role), 5) Hong Kong (route open but local pay data thin and the market competitive; unclear for Pakistanis).

### Inferences
